Credits

Credits & Rights

Every image, every song, every line — credited and licensable.

01

The rights statement

Every photograph, recording, composition, manuscript, and mark on this site is owned or controlled by Byron Jupiter and the credited rights holders named beside it.

Nothing here is public domain and nothing here is cleared for training, scraping, or resale. Use requires a written license.

Credit lines are part of the license. Where a photographer, writer, or publisher is named, that name travels with the work.

02

Who shot the archive

Cyril Darensbourg — Session & stage photography. Studio sessions, live rooms, collaborator portraits. Longtime collaborator on the writing side as well — credited on more of the catalog than any other co-writer.

Byron Jupiter — Archive photography. Console details, tape boxes, room documentation, field notes. Most of the archive's process imagery was shot from the operator's chair.

SBJ — Documentary photography. Behind-the-board coverage, campaign stills. Coverage of the working day: load-in, setup, takes, teardown.

Peggy and Stephanie Jupiter — Family archive. Family photographs, Jupiter Exxon and early-years imagery. Keepers of the pictures that predate the studio — the formative-ground record.

Credit line format: Photograph © 2026 [photographer] / Byron Jupiter Archive. Reproduction without the credit line is a breach of license.

03

Recording, mixing, and writing credits

Tending to My Garden (2024) — Recording engineer · Mix engineer. Performer: Aaniyah Anderson. Publisher: Good Delivery. Tracked and mixed in the room; vocal chain kept analog front to back.

I Can Be (2024) — Recording engineer · Mix engineer. Performer: Aaniyah Anderson. Publisher: Good Delivery. Companion session to “Tending to My Garden.”

1 Life 1 Family Radio (2010) — Writer · Producer. Performers: Byron Jupiter, Cyril Darensbourg. Publisher: Good Delivery. ISWC: T9055389667. Registered work in the BMI/ASCAP catalog; historical registry entry.

Marked up as structured data so search and answer engines attribute these roles correctly instead of guessing.

04

The registered works

163 works are registered to date — the registered fraction of the catalog, carried here as registry data. Publishing is administered through Big Red Nola Publishing, Good Delivery, Shirley Stone Publishing, and others.

The full searchable registry — titles, ISWC codes, writer and publisher splits — lives in the works registry.

05

Licensing the intellectual property

Editorial use — Press, journalism, and academic publication. One archive photograph or one manuscript excerpt, for a defined term. Photographer credit line required.

Sync & master — Quoted per use. Film, television, advertising, and game placements. Master and publishing cleared together where both are held. Stems and alternates supplied on approval.

Catalog & data — Structured access to the works registry (titles, ISWC, splits) and rights-holder reconciliation reporting. Suited to publishers, societies, and researchers. Terms are set in the written agreement.

Brand & institutional — Exhibition, brand campaign, and institutional programming. Photography, audio, manuscript, and mark system bundled, with co-branded credit and archival attribution. Terms are set in the written agreement.

Four ways the archive is licensed. Every tier carries a written agreement, a credit obligation, and a defined term.
