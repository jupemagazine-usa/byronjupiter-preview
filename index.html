The Person

He was in the room when New Orleans wrote its history — and made sure the room kept a copy.

Producer, engineer, archivist, planner, mentor, son and community builder. This is the working record of the music, people and systems carried forward through his hands.

The Foundation

The two people everything was built on.

Father · The name

Byron Jupiter Sr.

“My father was more than a road manager. He was the investment — his time, his energy, his money — and the presence: driving me to every event, standing at every one. That's why I carry his name. Byron Jupiter is his success as much as mine.”

Mother · The foundation

Peggy Jupiter

“My mother, Peggy Jupiter, was just as supportive. Married to my father, matched in devotion — her money kept me dressed, her time kept me fed, and her support held up everything in between. The wardrobe, the food, the foundation: hers.”
