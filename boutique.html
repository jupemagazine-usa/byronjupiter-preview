The Boutique

Silk · Monograms

The house goods: the monogram silks, the Monogram Alligator, Liquid Vatican — Fishing Silk, and the monogram family, designed in the house and placed by inquiry.

01

The Silks

Two squares, one design family: the Monogram Silk on obsidian, and the Monogram Silk on cream. Cut from silk — the material luxury products are made of. The BJ rides the center, the seals run the border, the woven field holds between.

02

The Goods

Every piece on this page was designed in the house. Nothing is priced in public; a piece is placed by inquiry, in writing.

03

The Monograms

Four marks ride the goods: the BJ fashion monogram, the B·O·X woven, the BX banded — the ring is the O — and the AMA, the mark of AMAnagement. Drawn in the house faces, Jupiter Deco and Jupiter Box.
