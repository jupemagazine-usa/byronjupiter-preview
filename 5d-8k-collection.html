The 5D / 8K Collection

Code · Render · Film

Film-bearing code, the engines that render it to film, and the documents built around the experience — the moving-image shelf of the house archive.

01

The 5D Experience

The lead picture of the collection, built from the house file minerva_5d_8k.html — recorded in full as “Minerva 5D // 8K Hyper-Dimensional Institutional Foundry”: an animated, dimensional foundry environment, written as a single file of code.

The film of the collection is built from this code in motion, rendered to film through the engines on this page.

02

GOING IN

“GOING IN: The 8K 3D Cinematic Experience” — a companion piece in the same code family, conceived as a film from the first line: the collection's second movement, standing beside the lead picture as its closing passage.

03

BREATHE 1.0

The render work that proved the method: a wordless short film directed by Byron Jupiter, rendered end to end from script to festival file. It plays at a breathing tempo — 71.26 BPM, swelling and settling by eight percent.

Entered at the Atlanta Film Festival 2027 (entry ATL27-5630). Its festival record stands with the film on the home page.

04

The Black Eye Recorder

The camera of the collection: the Black Eye HTML-to-Video Recorder, paired with VIDEO ENCODER v2.0. Together they capture the code pieces as they run and encode the capture as film.

05

HTML to MP4 Web Studio

The production bench: a studio environment for HTML-to-film work, carrying its technical dossier and an interactive museum build.

The museum build is the collection's exhibition surface.

06

THE NULL POINT

A motion-picture presentation of four and a half minutes, built around a 432Hz sound bed — a candidate inner movement of the film, and the collection's sound-design reference.

07

MINERVA V9

The MINERVA V9 Visual Master Deck — the companion document to THE NULL POINT, holding the presentation's visual record.

08

The 5D Experience Package

The documents built around the experience, made to travel with the film: the Experience Guide, the Viewer Card, the References Dossier, and the Learning Module.

The guide teaches the experience before a viewer enters it; the card is the light takeaway; the dossier holds the source of every claim; the module teaches the breath math the film is built on.
